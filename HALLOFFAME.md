# Hall of Fame

The people whose names the diskettes preserved.  Every entry is quoted
from the recovered bytes themselves — a banner, a title screen, a help
file — with the file it was read from.  Transliterated quotes are KOI-7
decoded back to Cyrillic.

## The authors

**Домнич А.В. (Александр)** — Воронеж / Каменка, 1994.  The most
prolific named engineer in the collection, at home in four languages:
the educational snake game «ПИТОН» (`UDAW.EXE`, Pascal, made to order
for И.В.Ф. «МИКРОТЕХ» — vowels eaten vertically, consonants
horizontally — protected by a self-integrity check that sums the first
1000 words of its own file and greets a tampered copy with «Привет
хакерам!!»); the perpetual calendar `CALEND.SAV` (built for the years
1583..5000, signed «Домнич А.В 16-06-94г.» in its source); `FCAD.SAV`
v1.1 — function graphs drawn to the ЭМ7052 plotter (one of the nine
graphics-mode programs of the cross); the Newton nonlinear-system
solver `NEWTON.FOR` («программист Домнич Александр»); and the
OUT17/OUTC printing utilities, whose docs end with his contact
line, «Подробнее :: Домнич А.В., тел. в Каменке».
> «Игру сделал Домнич Александр по заказу И.В.Ф. "МИКРОТЕХ". Воронеж 1994.»

**Воронков В.В.** — Воронеж, 1993-1995.  The keeper of the collection
and an author in it: the Pascal minesweeper `K.SAV` (1995) with its
whole workshop preserved in the logical disk `PROGS.DSK`, the BASIC sea
battle «МОРСКОЙ БОЙ» (`MORBOJ.BAS`, title screen `MORBOJ.SCR` signed
«VORONKOV COMPUTER'S SECURITY Ltd.»), the joystick space shooter
`LUNA.BAS` («PROGRAM BY VORONKOV SOFT 1993»), and the screen of a music editor
— a piano keyboard with the note frequencies in hertz, «COMPOSED BY
VORONKOV V.V. — VORONEZH 21-6-1993» (`MUZRED.SCR`).  The vvv104 disks
are his, and so is what on them no one else signed and no other hand
brought in a batch — the formula editor, the 3-D surface plotter, the
whole PROGS.DSK workshop: `programs/vvv/` in the collection.

**Родионов Сергей Алексеевич** — Воронеж, 1992-1993.  A whole private
operating environment: the RT15SJ monitor build («©1992 Родионов
С.А.»), the ROSA Commander v1.3 file manager (`ROSA3.SAV` — its status
line reads «ROSA Commander v1.3 © 1993 Родионов Сергей Алексеевич /
г.Воронеж /»: a two-panel DZ0:/DZ2: commander with a file-info box,
«Защищён от удаления» flags and a ПМ help key), his R15 edition of the
K13 screen editor with every prompt in Russian, the BLUE screen-and-date greeter — all wired into a boot that lands
straight in his own desktop.  His copy protection is two sectors on
physical track 0 of the back side, and his programs say so plainly:
> «Программа, переписанная без разрешения АВТОРА, не работает.» (ROSA3.SAV)

**В. Понимаш** — Львов, НИПП «Омега», 1992.  A developer of the
МС-0515 machine itself (as the collection's keeper attests) — and the
author of `ART.EXE`, the graphics editor whose screen the cross-run
measured at 96% ink: one of the machine's makers drawing on his own
canvas.
> «Версия 1.0.  Автор В.Понимаш.  ЛЬВОВ НИПП "Омега" 1992.»

**Борисов В.В.** — 1990.  The floppy formatter V2.1X (`2.SAV`):
Format/Check/Test, sectors 128..1024, 48/96 tpi — written for an
FPU-equipped PDP-11, and honest about it: its own banner carries
«Wrong Hardware or Software !», which is exactly what the МС-0515 is
for it.

**М. А. Еренбург** — the Towers of Hanoi (`HANOJ.SAV`), written for
the «Электроника НЦ 8020/2» (ДВК-2), with academic-style
acknowledgements baked into the program:
> «НАПИСАЛ М. А. ЕРЕНБУРГ.  ПРИ НАПИСАНИИ ПРОГРАММЫ АВТОР ПОЛУЧИЛ РЯД ЦЕННЫХ ЗАМЕЧАНИЙ ОТ Е.Г.БЕЛЯЕВА...»

**Салихов Л.В., школа 604** — `KOSMOS.SAV`, signed «(C) ABH corp.» —
a schoolchild with a corporation.
> «АВТОР: САЛИХОВ Л.В. ШК.604»

**Гостев Дмитрий** — the sprite generator `GENSPR.BAS`.
> «COPYRIGHT 1994 BY GOSTEV DMITRY»

**Грудзинский А.С.** — Львовский университет, the sprite program
`SPR.BAS`.
> «Львовского университета.  Автор Грудзинский А.С.»

**D. Climov** — the toolsmith of DEZI V05.01 (`DESS.SAV`, carried on
every one of the keeper's disks): an interactive octal dump viewer
with an ASCII gutter, pattern search, a stack line — and a live
«Macro-11:» disassembly of the word under the cursor.  The banner
signs itself «Originally written by D. Climov» with a phone number,
and «Originally» proved prophetic: `REDUMP.SAV` is the same binary
with the name hex-patched to «REDUMP B5.0e».

**Пажитнов А.Л.** — yes, that one.  The Tetris build `TET.SAV`
credits him on its title screen («ИГРА ПАЖИТНОВА А.Л.», KOI-7 in the
binary) and carries the Whitesmiths C runtime (© 1978) underneath.

**VLAD & ALEX** — «Sound Effect's Recorder version 2.0»: a home-made
4-bit sampler (playback through the MS7007 port B, recording through an
ADC on the printer port), its players `LD1.SAV` and `LOAD.SAV` («This is
MUPPET SHOW!», «European Top Twenty») and the full source set — and
three of their recordings survived (`A`, `9.PRG`, `TLF.PRG`), decodable
to recognizable sound today.

**Сторожевых С.В., 1988** — a driver author by name: the SL V08.00
single-line editor handler signs itself «SL V08.00 [SW] Сторожевых
С.В. 1988» inside `SL.SYS` — command recall at the monitor prompt,
eleven years before anyone would call that readline.

**«Некие парнишки» из Ростова-на-Дону, 1986** — deliberately
anonymous.  `SP49.SAV` tells its own origin story: a rework of the
«народная американская игра PAC-HALL», complete with a code of
conduct («чем хуже, тем лучше; наглость — второе счастье») and a
warning from experience:
> «Начальство отрицательно относится к подобного рода хобби (авторы познали это на своём горьком опыте).  Будьте осторожны!»

**Лицей №1 — the classroom** — the amk diskette is a school lab in
amber: pupil programs named after their makers (`IGOR.PAS` — the
magnetic-induction lab «Laborator», `LUDA10.PAS`, `HIM.PAS` —
«kislorod», the oxygen lab), all sharing one teacher's comment
template («РАЗДЕЛ КОНСТАНТ… РАЗДЕЛ ВНЕШНИХ ПРОЦЕДУР…»), a project
report (`ROK.TXT`) citing Мякишев-Буховцев's physics textbook — and
the lycée's own «Практическое руководство по языку PASCAL-РАФОС,
Лицей №1» in its bibliography.

## The software houses

**НИПП «Омега», Львов** — the professional heart of the machine's
software: the ОМЕГА SJ(S) V05.04 monitors, the БЕЙСИК-ОМЕГА
interpreter (`BASICO.SAV`; its manual: «РАЗРАБОТАН ЛЬВОВСКИМ
НАУЧНО-ИССЛЕДОВАТЕЛЬСКИМ ПРЕДПРИЯТИЕМ "ОМЕГА"»), the RT11/DOS/TR-DOS
file-system converter `FCON.EXE` («Экспериментальная версия Июль 1993
г.»), the SPRITE editor (`SPRED.DOC`, Львов 1991), ART.  «LWOW SOFT»'s
`EXPRES.SAV` («ITA EXPRESS», © 1990, joystick or keyboard) is the same
city at play.

**Mihin-soft & SPF «Сенсор», Воронеж, 1990** — the OS-16SJ monitor
(«Самые лучшие драйверы для УПБК!!!»), the FDZ diskette formatter for
УПБК-16 — and, uniquely in the collection, a postal address baked into
the program:
> «394043 г. Воронеж ул. Фронтовая 5.  Всегда буду рад.» (FDZ.SAV)

**EmeSoft, 1990** — «RS v06.05 RT11 profShell» (`RS.SYS`, build dated
13-Sep-94): a Norton-Commander-style disk shell packed into a 26-block
pseudo-device handler — a file panel with marks, the whole DUP/PIP
toolbox on hotkeys (up to BOOT and COPY/BOOT), LD containers, a
words/bytes/radix dump, and the greeting «Жми на клавишу, не бойся
...».  The System2/bg0515/superBAK7 and Buhgal monitor builds carry it
baked in — its banner opens their every boot.

**И.В.Ф. «МИКРОТЕХ», Воронеж** — commissioned «ПИТОН» (1994).

**ФМГ, Воронеж, 1994** — the «МУЗЫКАЛЬНЫЙ РЕДАКТОР» whose animated
advertisement (`ZASTM.EXE`) is all that survives of it.

**SB Soft Ware Ltd., 1992** — «AutoTeacher V3.10, Created by SB»
(`AT.SAV`) with its student-testing data files (`STUD.PUP`).

**Вычислительный центр АН СССР, Москва, 1987** — `DATIME.SAV`, the
date-and-time greeter the startup files run at every boot.

**РТК МИКРО, 1987-88** — the «технологический комплекс программиста»
whose documentation fills disk5: the РБД-МИКРО relational DBMS
(«разработана по Р-технологии программирования на языке Графический
Паскаль»), its command interpreter, and the programmer's menu shell —
serious software-engineering culture on a micro.

**Сберкасса, отделение 7503/0141** — not a software house but a
workplace: the PMK v1.2 teller station on the Buhgal disk, its
operational day interrupted on 03.04.02 and still resumable.

## The imported giants underneath

DEC (RT-11 V5.04 itself; SYSMAC.SML © 1986 Digital Equipment
Corporation), Electro-Scientific Industries (the OMSI Pascal-1
compiler, «COPYRIGHT C 1975,1976,1977», living on as `PAS1.SAV`),
Whitesmiths Ltd. (the C runtime of 1978 inside TET.SAV), and Robert
K. Jung (the ARJ archiver, whose manual the disks preserve in
Russian).
