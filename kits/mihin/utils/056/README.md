# The work diskette 056

Diskette `056` is the work diskette of the КВИ «Электроника МС0111» complex and carries no monitor, so it is no kit of its own.  Its three handlers are filed with this kit on proof — all of them carry the sysgen word `TIM$IT`, which of the collection's monitors only Mihin's OS-16SJ has (see [`../../handlers/056/`](../../handlers/056/README.md)).

This one program is filed here by that association and not by proof of its own: nothing in `DATIME.SAV` says which monitor it was meant for.  It is a different program from the `DATIME` of the `dec` and `vvv` kits — another author, another year.

The complex's own programs, its long manual and what the complex was are in [`../../../../programs/ms0111/`](../../../../programs/ms0111/README.md); the terminal emulator of that diskette is in `kits/omega/utils/`, the only kit whose monitor is on a disk that carried it.

|---|---|---|
| `DATIME.SAV` | DATIME «(C) 1987» of the ВЦ АН СССР - the greeter with escape-sequence highlighting | `RUN DATIME` |
