# Programs with their sources

The non-industrial half of the collection: everything home-made, one folder each, sources beside the binaries - `software/` keeps only the products that came without them.  `vvv/` - the collector's own programs (everything on his disks that no one else signed), with his minesweeper in its own folder; `domnich/` - Домнич's calendar, Newton solver and the snake game ПИТОН; `games-basic/` - the games in БЕЙСИК-ОМЕГА source; `covox/` - VLAD & ALEX's Covox-style digital audio (a sampler and its players, with three surviving recordings); `ms0111/` - the terminal complex that made the machine a terminal of an МС0108; `recode/` - the KOI-7 → KOI-8 filter in C; `muzred/` - the animated advert that is all that survives of ФМГ's music editor; `pictures/` - the screen dumps; `basic/` - the BASIC programs of the other disks that are not games; `pascal/` - Pascal programs of no known author that travelled with Домнич's; `examples/` - samples written for the collection's own disks, a first program to build.  The schoolroom is here too: `autoteacher/` - the AutoTeacher testing system with its question banks; `lyceum1/` - the coursework of Лицей №1, Воронеж.
