# VLAD & ALEX's digital audio

«Sound Effect's Recorder 2.0» — a 4-bit sampler: playback through the MS7007 PPI port B (177542, a Covox-style DAC), recording through a home-made ADC on the Centronics port.  `SER4.SAV` is the recorder, `LD1.SAV` and `LOAD.SAV` players; the full source set is here - and one recording survived: `A` (disk 056) is a 16K-word sample buffer in exactly their format, 65536 four-bit samples packed low nibble first, a smooth waveform of some eight seconds.  The emulator has no DAC yet, so the players run silent; decode the nibbles on the host to hear it.  `9.PRG` and `TLF.PRG` from the same disks are recordings in the players' other format - 8K words of one 8-bit sample each - and so are the eight `.PR` files of disk 066 (`100.PR` … `140.PR`, two seconds each).  All decode to sound on the host (4-bit nibbles low first, or the low byte of each word, at about 4 kHz - 8 kHz plays them twice too fast and a child's pitch).
