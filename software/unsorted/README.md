# Unsorted: the problem shelf

What the cross-run could not run, and the reads that failed - kept here, out of the folders proper, until each is examined.  The question for every item is the same: a bad read of the diskette, a build that wants something the probe disk did not have (a data file, a handler, memory, a system of its own kind), or a program that never could run on this machine.  Known so far:  `primer/` - PRIMER.SAV repeats «?Err 63 Illegal instruction trap» from its first instructions on every monitor, so its manual PRIMER.DOC is all that can be read of it;  `TET.SAV` - Пажитнов's Tetris on the Whitesmiths C runtime, traps through vector 10 at PC 022216 on every monitor - the one surviving copy may be damaged, or it wants an instruction the КР1807ВМ1 lacks (try it under GETEML/EM first);  `2.SAV` - Борисов's formatter opens with FP11 floating-point instructions (SETD/SETI) that this CPU has not and the EIS/FIS emulators do not cover - it was written for another PDP-11 and cannot run here;  `MSDOS.SAV` - exits at once, nothing identified inside;  `EDSP.SAV` (disk 063) - the frame editor's other copy, errors everywhere while the 062 copy runs, every block differs;  `EXPRES.SAV` (disk 066) - the train game's other copy, exits at once, 9 blocks differ from the running build;  `ked/` - three reads of KED.SAV from the collector's diskettes that fail while the restored images hold a running copy;  `PIP.SAV` - the vvv104 disk1 read of PIP, errors on 7 of 8 monitors while the other read of the same diskette runs.  The screens of every cross-run are in the emulator repository (`disk_recovery/work/cross3`, `viewer.py`).
